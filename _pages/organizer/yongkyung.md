## [YongKyung Oh](https://yongkyung-oh.github.io/)

Dr. YongKyung Oh is an Assistant Professor in the Data Science Major, School of BioMedical Convergence Engineering, Pusan National University. His research interests span AI for Science, industrial AI and decision-making, evidence-centered AI, and continuous-time modeling, with an emphasis on scientific discovery and informed decisions. He previously conducted postdoctoral research at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), from 2023 to 2026, and at the Ulsan National Institute of Science and Technology (UNIST). His earlier research experience includes General Motors (GM) and Carnegie Mellon University (CMU). He earned a B.Sc. in Physics, a Master in Technology and Innovation Management, and a Ph.D. in Industrial Engineering, all from UNIST. His recent work includes a sole-authored ICML 2026 position paper on evidence standards. His distinctions include the ICLR 2024 Spotlight, the CHIL 2025 Best Paper Award, and a nomination for the 2025 UCLA Chancellor's Award for Postdoctoral Research. His research support includes the NRF Postdoctoral Fellowship for Overseas Research as Principal Investigator and an NVIDIA Academic Grant as Lead Scientist.
