## [Sungil Kim](https://analytics.unist.ac.kr/)

Prof. Sungil Kim is an Associate Professor in the Department of Industrial Engineering at the Ulsan National Institute of Science and Technology (UNIST). His research addresses quality engineering problems in AI-enabled systems, including sensor drift, inefficient data representation, and class imbalance. His work uses sensor data and real-time log data for industrial analytics and decision support. Prof. Kim earned his Ph.D. degree in Industrial Engineering at the Georgia Institute of Technology in 2011. He has served as an Area Editor of Computers & Industrial Engineering in the area of Statistics, Quality, Reliability & Maintenance.
