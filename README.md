# NDE for TS

[Website](https://nde-for-ts.github.io/)

Resources on Neural Differential Equations for time series: Neural Ordinary Differential Equations (NODEs), Neural Controlled Differential Equations (NCDEs), and Neural Stochastic Differential Equations (NSDEs).

- [Review](https://nde-for-ts.github.io/review/): the IJCAI-25 survey of NDE formulations, theory, numerical methods, and benchmarks.
- [Tutorial](https://nde-for-ts.github.io/tutorial/): the CIKM 2025 tutorial, from mathematical foundations to practical examples.
- [Papers](https://nde-for-ts.github.io/papers/): a curated bibliography of NDE research.
- [Contributors](https://nde-for-ts.github.io/people/): the researchers behind these resources.
